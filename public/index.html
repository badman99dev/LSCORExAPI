REST & Streaming Endpoints
Method Route Description Action
SSE /matches/:id/stream Infinite SSE stream for a specific match. Sends initial snapshot then live diffs on every ball. On-Demand
GET /matches List all active and recent matches with live scores and logos. Open
GET /matches/live Filtered list of currently live matches. Open
GET /matches/:id Match details, striker/bowler stats with headshot images and jerseys. Detail
GET /matches/:id/squad Both teams' Playing XI and full squad with player photos and jerseys. Squad
GET /matches/:id/commentary Ball-by-ball commentary, shot types, wagons, and fall of wickets. Feed
GET /matches/:id/scorecard Structured scorecard breakdown for both innings. Scorecard
GET /stats Broadcaster metrics: active clients, watched matches, worker pool. View
GET /health Uptime & server health check. Health
